FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

What's available, what's in development, and how practical training stays legal and safe.

Is ZeroTrace Academy available today?

ZeroTrace Academy is being built in stages. Parts of the platform shown on this page, including the cyber range, the tools and the AI mentor, are previews of what's planned. Each one is labelled as a preview or as in development until it launches.

Are the ZeroTrace tools ready to use?

Not yet. The 24 tools listed here are proposed and in planning or development. We won't describe any of them as available until it is actually released.

Is the offensive security training legal?

Yes. All offensive techniques are taught for use only on systems you own or are explicitly authorised to test. Practical work runs in isolated lab environments, never against real third-party systems.

Do I need prior experience?

No. The Foundations departments and beginner learning paths assume no prior security knowledge. Each path lists its difficulty and suggested prerequisites.

Do you offer certificates?

We plan to issue completion records for paths and assessments. These are not accredited professional certifications, and we'll always describe them accurately.

What will the pricing be?

The plans shown are proposed and may change before launch. Lab access depends on plan eligibility and fair-use limits; we do not offer unlimited virtual machines.
