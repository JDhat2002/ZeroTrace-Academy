Account recovery

Get back into your account

Recovery codes are created when two-factor authentication is turned on. Each code works once. ZeroTrace does not email those codes.

  • Forgot the password: request a reset. The reply does not reveal whether the email is registered.
  • Still have a recovery code: enter it on the verification screen in place of the authenticator code.
  • Lost both the app and the codes: reset the password, then contact support from a channel you already trust. Support cannot read your password.
